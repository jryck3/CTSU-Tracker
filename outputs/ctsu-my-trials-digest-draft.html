Entry. Early triple-negative breast cancer, cT2-4 N0, cT1-3 N1-2, or TX N1-2, M0 (no T4/N+, N3, or inflammatory)
Stratification. Nodal status negative vs positive (TIL status used as an analysis stratum)
Randomize 1:1. Neoadjuvant chemo-immunotherapy, starting within 21 days
Arm 1: Carboplatin/paclitaxel/pembrolizumab then AC/pembrolizumab Cycles 1-4 (21 days): paclitaxel 80 mg/m2 days 1, 8, 15 plus carboplatin AUC 1.5 weekly or AUC 5 day 1, plus pembrolizumab 200 mg every 3 weeks. Cycles 5-8: doxorubicin 60 mg/m2 + cyclophosphamide 600 mg/m2 every 21 or 14 days, pembrolizumab every 3 weeks (8 neoadjuvant doses)
Arm 2: Carboplatin/docetaxel/pembrolizumab (anthracycline-free) Docetaxel 75 mg/m2 + carboplatin AUC 6 + pembrolizumab 200 mg day 1 every 21 days x 6 cycles with required growth factor
Surgery. Definitive surgery recommended 3-10 weeks after last neoadjuvant dose
Adjuvant. Pembrolizumab recommended for all (total 55 weeks). pCR: no further chemotherapy. Residual disease: Arm 1 capecitabine at physician discretion; Arm 2 AC x 4 with pembrolizumab at physician discretion, with or without capecitabine; olaparib allowed for germline BRCA. Radiation per standard guidelines
Follow-up. 5 years from registration
Primary endpoint. Breast cancer event-free survival (non-inferiority of Arm 2)

1,733 of 2,400 accrued (72.2%). open since Sep 2023. 47 per month on average. projected Dec 2027 at that rate

BRIGHT (S2427)

Phase 2 · SWOG · Open · NCT07061964

Single Arm Phase II Study of Bladder Preservation with Immunoradiotherapy After a Clinically Meaningful Response to Neoadjuvant Therapy in Patients with Muscle Invasive Bladder Cancer (BRIGHT)

Genitourinary Cancers

Muscle-invasive disease. Histologic cT2-T4aN0 urothelial carcinoma of the bladder.
Neoadjuvant therapy. Physician's choice, limited to the FDA-approved regimens in the eligibility criteria.
Response assessment. cT0 or cT1 on a repeat TURBT with systematic biopsies. That TURBT is within 90 days before registration.
Registration. Within 90 days after the last neoadjuvant dose.
Treatment. Bladder radiotherapy plus pembrolizumab for 1 year. Central radiation-plan approval is required before treatment starts. Radiotherapy is whole bladder only, 55 Gy in 20 fractions of 2.75 Gy, with no pelvic nodes and no tumor boost.
On-study assessment. Cystoscopy, cytology, and imaging.
Endpoint. 3-year bladder-intact event-free survival. Follow-up continues for 5 years after registration.

56 of 111 accrued (50.5%). open since Nov 2025. 5.3 per month on average. projected Aug 2027 at that rate
