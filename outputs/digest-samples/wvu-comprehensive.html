Neoadjuvant therapy. Physician's choice, limited to the FDA-approved regimens in the eligibility criteria.
Response assessment. cT0 or cT1 on a repeat TURBT with systematic biopsies. That TURBT is within 90 days before registration.
Registration. Within 90 days after the last neoadjuvant dose.
Treatment. Bladder radiotherapy plus pembrolizumab for 1 year. Central radiation-plan approval is required before treatment starts. Radiotherapy is whole bladder only, 55 Gy in 20 fractions of 2.75 Gy, with no pelvic nodes and no tumor boost.
On-study assessment. Cystoscopy, cytology, and imaging.
Endpoint. 3-year bladder-intact event-free survival. Follow-up continues for 5 years after registration.
