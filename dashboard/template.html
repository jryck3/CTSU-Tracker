        Data sources and methods
        • Accrual comes from the public CTSU Protocol List (ctsu.cancer.gov/protocol), which lists active and temporarily closed NCI trials open to cross-group participation and reports actual over planned intervention accrual. These are study-wide totals across all sites, not accrual at any one institution.
        • History is built from one CTSU snapshot per day, starting . CTSU does not publish past accrual, so the line from the study start date to the first snapshot is a straight-line interpolation (dashed), not observed data.
        • Average rate = current accrual divided by months since the actual study start date on ClinicalTrials.gov. Early ramp-up makes this lower than the current rate for most young trials. Recent rate = change over the last 30 days of snapshots, scaled to a month. Projected target date assumes the average rate continues.
        • Accrual pace ranks open studies that match the explorer filters, have been open at least 6 months, and have not reached their target. Patients per month favors large trials; % of target per month adjusts for trial size.
        • RT classification uses ClinicalTrials.gov interventions and titles. RT-focused: RT named in the title. RT component: protocol-specified RT intervention (often part of the treatment backbone). Radiopharmaceutical: therapeutic isotope (for example Lu-177, Ac-225) without external-beam RT. Imaging tracers and MUGA scans are excluded. Manual overrides are listed in the evidence for each study.

        Independent project, not affiliated with or endorsed by the National Cancer Institute, the Cancer Trials Support Unit (CTSU), or the U.S. National Library of Medicine. Accrual comes from the public CTSU Protocol List; study details come from ClinicalTrials.gov, courtesy of the U.S. National Library of Medicine. Data are refreshed once a day and may lag the sources. Intended for clinicians and research teams, not for individual patient care decisions.
